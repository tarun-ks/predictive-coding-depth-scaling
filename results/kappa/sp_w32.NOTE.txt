Standard-parameterization spectrum: why this file stops at L=32.
=================================================================

This run requested depths 4,8,16,32,64. It completed 4-32 and aborted at L=64
when the shifted-Lanczos solve failed to converge. The completed rows are in
sp_w32.csv; the abort is recorded below rather than discarded, because the
failure is itself a result: under the standard parameterization lambda_min falls
fast enough that it stops being resolvable.

L=64 was subsequently resolved in a separate run with a larger Krylov basis
(ncv=200); see results/kappa/sp_64.csv. L=128 was not resolvable by any
setting we tried. Local paths below rewritten to <repo>.
